Delete Your Tabibak Account

If you want to delete your Tabibak account and associated personal data, you can submit an account deletion request.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Tabibak account and associated data, please contact the Tabibak support team.

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • The phone number or email address associated with your account
  • Your request to delete your Tabibak account

What Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete the personal data associated with your account, except for information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Uninstalling the App

Important: Uninstalling the Tabibak application from your device does not delete your Tabibak account or the data associated with your account.

To delete your account and associated data, you must submit an account deletion request using the process described above.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Tabibak handles user data, please visit our Privacy Policy .

Contact

For account deletion requests or questions about your personal data, please contact the Tabibak support team.
